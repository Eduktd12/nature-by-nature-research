# Nature by Nature Research Portfolio

Nature by Nature Research is a Zambia-based scientific platform working across molecular biosciences, plant genetics, natural products, mycology, fermentation systems, industrial hemp, *Cannabis sativa*, diagnostics, and evidence-informed regulatory science.

## Core capabilities

### Molecular biology and genetics
- Plant DNA-based characterization
- Molecular-marker and genotyping workflows
- Germplasm diversity studies
- Population-level genetic characterization
- Targeted molecular assays
- Laboratory implementation and method development

### Industrial hemp
- Hemp genetics and molecular diversity
- Low-THC/compliant germplasm research
- Crop-development questions linking genotype, phenotype and agronomy
- Fibre, seed and multipurpose hemp research opportunities
- Scientific support for evidence-based hemp regulation and industry development

### Cannabis science
- *Cannabis sativa* genetics and molecular characterization
- Chemotype-oriented research
- Cannabinoid-related scientific questions
- Germplasm diversity and population structure
- Research interfaces between laboratory evidence, regulation and public policy

### Mycology
- Mushroom and fungal biodiversity
- Molecular identification and fungal genetics
- Applied mycology
- Natural-product and ecological interfaces

### Fermentation, SCOBY and microbial consortia
- Kombucha and SCOBY systems
- Fermentation biology
- Microbial-community research
- Product-development science
- Natural fermentation and translational bioscience

### Natural products and translational bioscience
- African biological resources
- Plant- and fungus-derived natural-product research
- Analytical and characterization questions
- Product-development pathways grounded in scientific evidence

### Diagnostics and laboratory science
- Molecular diagnostics
- Laboratory workflow design
- Targeted assays and implementation research
- Quality-oriented scientific documentation

### Regulatory and legal-scientific research
- Evidence-informed cannabis and hemp policy
- Controlled-substance science
- Research governance and ethics
- Scientific evidence in regulatory and legal decision-making
- Biodiversity and natural-resource governance

## Collaboration profile

Nature by Nature is particularly interested in collaborations that:

- involve Southern African biological diversity or research infrastructure;
- generate publishable molecular, genomic, ecological or analytical evidence;
- improve laboratory capacity, reproducibility or local scientific capability;
- support responsible industrial hemp and cannabis development;
- connect mycology, fermentation, natural products and biotechnology;
- bridge scientific evidence with law, regulation, public health or policy;
- create opportunities for African students, laboratories and researchers.

## Geographic emphasis

Primary base: **Lusaka, Zambia**  
Regional emphasis: **Southern Africa**  
Wider scope: **African and international scientific collaboration**

## Public-safe publishing policy

Only public, non-confidential material is published in this repository. Unpublished sequence data, private sample information, confidential protocols, grant drafts, restricted documents, partner-confidential information and patent-sensitive material are excluded.
