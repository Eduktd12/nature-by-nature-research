# Mycology, Fermentation and SCOBY Research

Nature by Nature Research is developing a multidisciplinary research stream spanning fungi, mushrooms, fermentation systems, microbial consortia and natural-product applications.

## Mycology

Research interests include:

- fungal and mushroom diversity
- molecular identification of fungi
- fungal genetics
- applied mycology
- ecological and ethnobiological questions
- fungus-derived natural products
- African mushroom biodiversity and under-characterized taxa

## Fermentation and SCOBY systems

Research interests include:

- kombucha and SCOBY systems
- mixed microbial communities
- fermentation biology
- microbial ecology
- product-development research
- fermentation-linked natural products
- reproducibility and characterization of artisanal biological systems

## Translational direction

This research stream links fundamental biology with biotechnology, product development, microbial ecology, food and beverage science, natural products and locally relevant innovation.

## Collaboration

Nature by Nature welcomes collaborations with mycologists, microbiologists, fermentation scientists, food scientists, natural-product researchers, molecular laboratories and biotechnology groups.
