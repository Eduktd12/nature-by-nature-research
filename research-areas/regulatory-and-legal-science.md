# Regulatory and Legal-Scientific Research

Nature by Nature Research works at the interface of laboratory evidence, public policy, research governance and law.

## Areas of interest

- evidence-informed cannabis and industrial hemp regulation
- controlled-substance science and policy
- scientific evidence in regulatory decision-making
- research ethics and governance
- biodiversity and natural-resource governance
- laboratory standards and evidentiary reliability
- public-health interfaces
- law-science communication

## Approach

The objective is not legal advocacy for a predetermined outcome, but disciplined translation of scientific evidence into regulatory, institutional and policy contexts. This includes identifying where scientific uncertainty, laboratory limitations, sampling design, analytical validity or biological variability may affect decisions.

## Collaboration

Potential collaborators include universities, regulators, policy institutes, laboratories, public-health researchers, legal scholars and interdisciplinary research programmes.
