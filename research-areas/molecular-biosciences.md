# Molecular Biosciences and African Genetic Resources

Nature by Nature Research develops and supports molecular-bioscience work centered on African biological diversity, practical laboratory implementation and publishable scientific evidence.

## Research interests

- plant genetics and genomics
- molecular markers and genotyping
- germplasm characterization
- population diversity and structure
- targeted molecular assays
- molecular identification
- crop and biodiversity genetics
- laboratory workflow development
- molecular diagnostics
- translational molecular research

## African research emphasis

A central objective is to improve the scientific visibility of under-characterized African plant, fungal and microbial resources while maintaining appropriate governance, permissions, attribution and intellectual-property safeguards.

## Collaboration opportunities

Nature by Nature welcomes collaborations involving universities, molecular laboratories, crop programmes, biodiversity researchers, diagnostics groups and research networks seeking a Southern African research partner.
