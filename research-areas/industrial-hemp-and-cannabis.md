# Industrial Hemp and Cannabis Research

Nature by Nature Research maintains distinct but complementary research interests in **industrial hemp** and ***Cannabis sativa*** science.

## Industrial hemp

Public-facing research interests include:

- hemp genetics and molecular diversity
- compliant low-THC germplasm
- crop improvement and genotype–phenotype questions
- fibre, seed and multipurpose hemp systems
- agronomy-linked molecular characterization
- African hemp germplasm and regional adaptation
- evidence generation for hemp standards, regulation and industry development

## Cannabis science

Research interests include:

- *Cannabis sativa* population genetics
- molecular characterization of germplasm
- chemotype differentiation
- cannabinoid-related research questions
- genetic diversity and population structure
- molecular markers and targeted assays
- African cannabis diversity and under-characterized germplasm
- laboratory evidence relevant to medicinal, scientific and regulatory questions

## Scientific approach

The emphasis is on reproducible molecular and analytical work, responsible handling of regulated material, public-safe communication, and collaborations with clear permissions, authorship, data-governance and intellectual-property boundaries.

## Collaboration

Potential collaborators may include universities, plant breeders, regulated producers, laboratories, agricultural programmes, analytical facilities, public-health researchers and regulatory-science groups.
