# Nature by Nature Research

**African molecular biosciences, plant genetics, industrial hemp and *Cannabis sativa* science, mycology, fermentation, diagnostics, natural products, and evidence-informed regulatory research.**

Nature by Nature Research is an independent scientific research and collaboration platform based in Lusaka, Zambia, with a Southern African and wider African research focus. The programme connects molecular biology, biodiversity, laboratory science, translational research, and science-informed policy.

## Research portfolio

| Research area | Public-facing scope |
|---|---|
| **Molecular genetics & genomics** | Plant genetics, molecular markers, genotyping, germplasm diversity, targeted molecular assays, population-level characterization |
| **Industrial hemp science** | Hemp genetics, crop improvement, compliant low-THC germplasm, fibre/seed research, agronomy-linked molecular characterization |
| ***Cannabis sativa* research** | Cannabis genetics, chemotype characterization, cannabinoid-related research, molecular diversity, germplasm and evidence generation |
| **Mycology & fungal biology** | Fungal diversity, mushroom research, fungal genetics, molecular identification and applied mycology |
| **Fermentation & SCOBY systems** | Microbial consortia, kombucha/SCOBY research, fermentation biology, product-development research and microbial ecology |
| **Natural products & analytical science** | African biological resources, phytochemistry-facing research, natural-product characterization and translational product science |
| **Molecular diagnostics** | Practical molecular assays, laboratory implementation, quality-oriented workflows and research diagnostics |
| **Regulatory & legal-scientific research** | Evidence-informed regulation, controlled-substance science, research governance, scientific policy and law-science interfaces |
| **Research networks & capacity building** | Southern African scientific collaboration, researcher matching, laboratory networks, student development and translational partnerships |

## Priority themes

### Industrial hemp and cannabis

Industrial hemp and medicinal/scientific cannabis are treated as related but distinct research streams. Nature by Nature supports public-safe work on *Cannabis sativa* genetics, chemotypes, molecular characterization, germplasm diversity, compliant hemp crop development, and scientifically grounded regulatory questions.

See: [`research-areas/industrial-hemp-and-cannabis.md`](research-areas/industrial-hemp-and-cannabis.md)

### African molecular biosciences

A major focus is the molecular characterization of under-represented African biological diversity, especially plant genetic resources and research questions that can generate publishable datasets, improved laboratory methods, locally relevant diagnostics, or crop and biodiversity knowledge.

See: [`research-areas/molecular-biosciences.md`](research-areas/molecular-biosciences.md)

### Mycology, fermentation and SCOBY research

The portfolio includes fungal biology, mushroom research, microbial consortia, fermentation, kombucha/SCOBY systems, and interdisciplinary work linking molecular biology with natural products and product-development science.

See: [`research-areas/mycology-fermentation-and-scoby.md`](research-areas/mycology-fermentation-and-scoby.md)

### Regulatory science and law-science interfaces

Nature by Nature also works at the boundary between scientific evidence, laboratory realities, regulation, public health, biodiversity governance, controlled-substance policy, and research ethics.

See: [`research-areas/regulatory-and-legal-science.md`](research-areas/regulatory-and-legal-science.md)

## Geographic and collaboration focus

The programme is rooted in **Zambia and Southern Africa**, while remaining open to African and international collaboration. Relevant collaboration areas include:

- African plant genetics, genomics and germplasm characterization
- industrial hemp breeding, genetics and compliant crop development
- *Cannabis sativa* genetics, chemotype and molecular research
- molecular biology, diagnostics and laboratory implementation
- mycology, fungal genetics and mushroom science
- fermentation, SCOBY and microbial-consortia research
- natural products and translational bioscience
- biodiversity and conservation-linked molecular research
- evidence-informed cannabis/hemp regulation and controlled-substance science
- research-network development, training and scientific capacity building

## Public-data and IP policy

This repository contains only material that is safe for public release. It does **not** publish unpublished sample-level datasets, private sequences or primers, proprietary protocols, confidential collaborator information, reviewer correspondence, patentable methods, grant-application text, or unpublished results requiring institutional or co-author approval.

Scientific visibility is pursued without compromising intellectual property, participant confidentiality, regulatory obligations, collaborator rights, or future publication.
