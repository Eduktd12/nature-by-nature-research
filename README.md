# Nature by Nature Research

Independent scientific research and collaboration hub focused on African molecular biology, plant genetics and genomics, natural products, mycology, diagnostics, and evidence-informed regulatory science.

## Research focus

- African plant genetic resources and germplasm diversity
- *Cannabis sativa* / industrial hemp genetics, chemotype and molecular characterization
- Plant genomics, molecular markers and targeted molecular assays
- Mycology and fungal genetics
- Natural products and analytical science
- Molecular diagnostics and laboratory implementation
- Public health, controlled-substance science and regulatory policy
- African research collaboration, capacity building and translational science

## Current scientific direction

The programme emphasizes under-represented African biological diversity, reproducible molecular characterization, practical laboratory methods, and collaborations that can translate local datasets into publishable science, research infrastructure, policy evidence and future funding.

## Collaboration

We welcome serious academic, laboratory, institutional and industry collaboration where the scientific scope, permissions, authorship, data governance and intellectual-property boundaries are clear.

Potential collaboration areas include:

- plant genomics and crop improvement
- germplasm characterization
- hemp and *Cannabis sativa* research
- molecular biology and diagnostics
- mycology
- natural-product chemistry
- African research networks
- scientific and regulatory policy

## Public-data and IP policy

This repository contains only material that is safe for public release. It does **not** publish unpublished sample-level datasets, private sequences or primers, proprietary protocols, confidential collaborator information, reviewer correspondence, patentable methods, grant-application text, or unpublished results requiring institutional or co-author approval.

## Repository structure

- `public-projects/` — high-level descriptions of public-safe research initiatives
- `publications/` — links and metadata for published or formally public outputs
- `resources/` — non-confidential templates, public methods notes and reference material
- `collaboration/` — public collaboration interests and contact information
